Preparation & Storage

Simple tips for enjoying and storing papadam.

🔥 Preparation

Heat oil properly before frying. Fry papadam briefly until it expands and becomes crisp. Avoid overheating the oil.

🥡 Storage

Keep uncooked papadam in a cool, dry place. Close the pack tightly after opening and protect it from moisture.

🍽️ Serving

Serve freshly prepared papadam with rice, curry or your favourite meal.
