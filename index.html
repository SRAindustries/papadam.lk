Preparation & Storage

Simple tips for enjoying crispy papadam.

🍳 How to Prepare

Heat oil properly before frying. Fry papadam briefly until it expands and becomes crispy. Remove carefully and allow excess oil to drain.

📦 How to Store

Keep uncooked papadam in its sealed packaging in a cool, dry place. Protect it from moisture to maintain freshness and quality.
