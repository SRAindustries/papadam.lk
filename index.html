Preparation & Storage

🍳 Preparation

Heat oil properly. Fry papadam briefly until it expands and becomes crispy. Remove carefully and drain excess oil.

📦 Storage

Keep uncooked papadam in sealed packaging in a cool, dry place. Protect it from moisture.
