Preparation & Storage

Simple tips to enjoy crispy papadam.

🍳 Preparation

Heat sufficient oil in a suitable pan. Fry papadam briefly until it expands and becomes crispy. Remove carefully and allow excess oil to drain. Serve fresh.

📦 Storage

Keep papadam in a cool, dry place. Store in an airtight container after opening. Protect from moisture to help maintain freshness and crispness.
